The time it takes to make the cloth
To twist the rope, and press the oakum in
The wind is worth the catching
The breeze is laughing, rising fast
Into a sky the blue of days
The tone of bells down the hallway
Make peace, make art, make love
On teak, mahogany, and oak
Whitewash the stars and bless
The wake of clouds that skein 
The dark behind the sky

Pull back your hair and balance
Easy while time take it easy
Your bones relax, your scars
Forget their song and salty
Wind taunts the catch

If this combination
Of wind and sun and water
Inebriates some truths
Mom knew that the drug was worth the taking
