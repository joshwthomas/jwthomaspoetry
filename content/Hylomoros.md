Slowly walking through the aisles  
grab the last thing on her list,  
when the trees accelerated:  
forest fear with faded myths.

Roots ruptured through the gleaming floor;  
the speed of growth one hundred fold.  
Muscular branch of ancient cedar,  
scaled and frosted punching upwards,  
like the slow gasp of human lungs.  
High above the commerce floor,  
twiggy fingers grasping the tube,  
fluorescent shattering to powder,  
letting darkness return.

Upheaving loamy scented soil,  
granite cracking through the aisles.  
Not the first time, there were warnings,  
Night staff repaired the damage.

There were warnings in the park,  
the wind that came from branches moving.  
There were warnings in the dark,  
the boles that moved in closer,  
disrupting the foundations,  
tenderizing infrastructure,  
preparing for the take-back,  
_revolution of the green._  
Knowing ants crawling up canyons  
Between those heavy scales.

She trying not to show the growing  
fear or glance over her shoulder,  
not staring at blinded lights  
buried under upward weight  
Of those veiny hardened leaves.

A gradient of blackness laying  
across the grocery floors.  
Light creeping away on spider's legs.  
All her poor eyes receive  
is a faintness of sky where  
Trees can’t clasp over the roof hole,  
before she’s swallowed by the woods,  
before balance is truly lost.
