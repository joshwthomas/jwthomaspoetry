# Thoughts about the Book
It would build trust if you could read her as a poet before you read her as a guide. 

[At Black River by Mary Oliver](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poetrymagazine/browse?volume=182&issue=5&page=33)

I think this is such a great poem for a couple of clear reasons. On page 65 of the book, Mary Oliver describes a poem called [The Fish by Marianne Moore](https://poets.org/poem/fish-1). The form of the poem The Fish uses the exact same syllables in each line, which creates a form that promises something, and then delivers. Mary Oliver doesn't use the same format - her syllables vary from line to line, but the enjambment of the poem serves the same function - it promises, and delivers, creating pleasure. 

Nothing I can say is going to build much on the book, because it's concise, well thought out, and wise. I keep coming back to it for inspiration when I am running aground, trying to capture something. I think it's very much worth reading.
