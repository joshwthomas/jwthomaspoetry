Something crawls through the long grass 
Towards the darkness in my tent
I wake up from fitful dreaming
It goes silent 
I lean upwards
Hauling off my sheets

It crawls along the side of the vestibule 
That keeps the dew from forming on my face
I kick out at the side of my tent gently
The unseen creature scurries 
Into the silence 

I close my eyes to fall asleep again
Trying to douse the fire 
Of the questions in my head
Giving up worry because 
I've seen these things before

Then it's chewing, chewing, chewing
Rustling so near my head 
I can't help but sit bolt upright
Wondering whether I should 
Tear out of my tent
Stomp loud enough to scare the creature
Insect, snake, or little mink 
Like the ones I saw crossing the road
Coming back from the river

Instead I kick out where I think it might be
In the starlight prickled darkness
A little crescent moonlight diffused 
Through the tent cloth
Now I am more awake than I have ever been
I remember I left out a sandwich in a bag
Then the creature is silent, I must have made 
My point, it is so quiet for so long,
I decide it's best to get as much sleep as 
A lonely camper can

Then it's crawling between the netting 
Revealing it's form - a field mouse
The size of my hand above me, 
Staring down at me, recusingly 
As if it wants to say I left the sandwich 
So I should be at peace to let it eat its dinner
Without such rude host manners
It scurries suddenly, above my face
I scootch sideways 
In case it finds a way to get much closer

No telepathy between us
Though the bridge of air is small
We star at each other
My heart's beating fast
But the mouse seems calm
With a flicker of fast movement
Mouse vanishes from sight

It's quiet for two minutes
Before the cycle begins again
But this time Mouse has a plan
To catch me laying there
Just as soon as I swing out
A slow foot to chase away
He slips underneath my tent floor
Thin fabric and a half full bedroll
Mouse is scurrying underneath my back
Thrill of electric jolt through me
I try to turn and wiggle
But I can't stay away

I make my move so fast, 
Hurtling out of the zippered tent
Then I turn at stare at it
Like the mouse will chase me out

Then I walk away, down the field into the night
Past the other people's tents
Who I have probably woken up
I walk about two minutes
In the chilly misty night
An unknown hour, a silly throb
Of an unidentified emotion

Then I gather all the things 
That I left beside the tent
Except the sandwhich I can't see
Because I will not use a light
When I have things arranged
When the car door light dims again
I see across my tent
That the other zipper is ajar
