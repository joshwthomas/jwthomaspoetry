I helped write typewriter poetry at Farm Aid 2026. 

On Saturday, September 26, 2026, at the Veterans United Home Loans Amphitheater in Virginia Beach, Virginia, I helped @poemsforpeopleva, with Ashley Logan Hill. We collected around 170 requests for poems at the exhibitors area in the field behind the amphitheater. 

The shows were incredible, the exhibitions were great, and the weather was iffy, cold and sprinkly.

Here's a series of poems that I wrote for Farm Aid 2026.
