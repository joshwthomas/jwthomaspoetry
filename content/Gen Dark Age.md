You don't know we're sliding towards 
another Dark Age, he said.
You believe in the world as it is, he said.  
Your persistence is a screen;  
The trees are aflame;  
The waves are getting taller;  
The economy must fall into the sea.

For us, our serenity prayer  
Is an unregulated vape, and
Bedrotting with social media.
Because we can't get jobs,  
And it's a day's wages  
To see our friends in person.

The widening gyre twists my insides.  
The suppression of progress will probably hold.  
He wants to sleep in his hand me down car tonight,  
On the city streets, because  
He'll deliver people's 
groceries from an app tomorrow.

My youngest son agrees.  
Strangled in the rising confusion,  
Magnified by tools  
We don't try to understand  
The enlightenment struggled to breath  
Along with the covid millions  
The line graph of the spread  
A readout for another, new cascade

They tell me the erosion of our collective power  
As a state, nation and globe  
To address new issues will defer  
Personal developments for them  
And the effects keep growing  
Interacting like rogue waves

My jaw is ajar, my eyes are open wide.  
I listen to their words and wonder how 
I could have lied to myself:
Genius systems can predict the cycle.  
But the graph of multiplying deaths  
Is a broken statue, vast thighs broken at the top  
Indignant birds wheel by.  
Look on my works, ye mighty - 
Graph of the Covid spread:
Banner headline for 
The new Dark Age.
There was a time we tamed falcons  
Like that one, but not this time.

My middle child is the vanguard  
Of Gen Dark Age, copping styles  
From the failed cultural revolution,  
Blunted renaissances taking solace 
From displaced Marxists.  
Coping with youtube revisionist history,
Real cigarettes on the stoop.
Laughing at all the channels of agit-prop  
Poured into his feeds.

The gifts the Dark Age brings  
Are like a touch of grey.  
The simple life - community customs  
Honed by moats and walls.  
When your wrist watch battery dies,  
Time will be uncaged.
We'll build a bridge of cut stones  
To let new pilgrims climb.
Touch grass with our frenemies,
Drink the local wine.
In the gloaming light, 
No tickets for the ren faire:  
You'll go there to sell wool.
Friends spin their quilts 
Cover their children's beds, 
Old lessons fresh relearned.
