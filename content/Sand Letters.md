Reddish quiet sand between the stones
sweep like letters scrolling down the river 
but I flow on the current too close to
be literate or remember what it says

long locks of green grasses stare me 
down tips gliding through the surface
vees ripple from each tip like letters
but I flow on the surface too close to
let the memories flood my mind

patterns flocking little waves moving in lock
step crossed by manic motion of subtle smaller
flocks of lines of tiny currents in 
the square foot my eyes are locked on
without the light shattered correctly
over the surface of the thin sheet
I would never see it 
or have it written inside me

it is happening now the writing 
of the river flowing and cascading falling 
into the sounds of sunset
but i cannot see it and i never will remember
until i float again still too close to read
